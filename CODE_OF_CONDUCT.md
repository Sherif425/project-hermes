# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to a respectful, inclusive, constructive environment for collaborators and readers of Project Hermes.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate professionally and give constructive feedback.
- Credit sources and respect intellectual-property rights.
- Protect privacy and sensitive technical information.
- Welcome learners at different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, or disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
