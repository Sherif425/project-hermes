# Quality and Safety Standards

Every Hermes asset should pass this checklist before publication.

## Accuracy

- Technical claims are supported by tested work or trustworthy cited sources.
- Commands, code, and architecture diagrams match the described version and context.
- Limitations, trade-offs, and failures are stated where relevant.

## Safety and privacy

- No passwords, tokens, API keys, certificates, private endpoints, or real customer data.
- Screenshots and logs are reviewed for sensitive details.
- Examples use sanitized or synthetic data.
- Security-sensitive guidance is framed responsibly and does not enable misuse.

## Clarity and professionalism

- The intended audience and desired outcome are clear.
- The title, introduction, and conclusion accurately reflect the content.
- Sources, tools, and collaborators receive appropriate attribution.
- Links and code examples have been checked.

## Reusability

- The source Atlas or Orion milestone is linked.
- The asset is categorized and recorded after publication.
- Material is licensed appropriately before public release.
